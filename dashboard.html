Help & Support

How do I start reading a script?

Tap Read on any script. Press the big play button. Drag the text with your finger to move manually, or use ↑ ↓ to change speed.

How can I add section bookmarks?

In the editor tap Add bookmark – it inserts a line like [[Intro]]. In the teleprompter open the bookmark menu to jump to it.

Where are my scripts stored?

On this device always. If you connect Google Drive, also in a “GD SCRIPT” folder in your own Drive, so you can use them on other devices.

Voice Follow is not working

It needs Chrome, Edge or Safari, internet, microphone permission and a speech language that matches your script. Roman-script Hindi/Bengali (typed in English letters) cannot be matched.

Can I use a Bluetooth clicker or keyboard?

Yes. Page Down / Page Up = next / previous paragraph, Space = play / pause, arrows = speed. Press ? in the teleprompter for the full list.
