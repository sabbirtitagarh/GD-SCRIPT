What is stored where: scripts and settings are kept in this browser (IndexedDB / localStorage). With Google Drive connected, scripts are also saved in your own Drive folder. Danger zone

Deleting your account removes your login, branding and settings from the server. Scripts already saved in your own Google Drive are not deleted.
